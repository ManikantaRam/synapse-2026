Event overview

Three rounds. Ideas into action.

SYNAPSE is a three-round corporate strategy and innovation fest where teams pitch, analyse business challenges, and build a proof of concept through a basic prototype. Event date and venue will be announced later.

ROUND 01
↗

Founder's Arena

Startup pitching and idea evaluation. Teams present their thinking and make their case in the elimination round.

ROUND 02
▥

Corporate Autopsy

Revive a failed company through strategic thinking. Analyse what went wrong and propose a path forward.

ROUND 03
⌘

Technobuild

Turn your company concept into a proof of concept. Define the core idea and build a basic working prototype, such as a simple website, to demonstrate how it could work.
